Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · Applies to: Colors of the Day for iPhone

In short: Colors of the Day works entirely on your iPhone. The app has no account, makes no server connection and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking. Your games and statistics stay on your device, and Flux76 receives no data from the app.

1. Who we are

Colors of the Day is published by Flux76, an independent software studio in the Netherlands (“Flux76”, “we”, “us”). For any privacy question or request, email support@flux76.com.

2. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following on your iPhone only. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Your game history: for each day you play, the five target colours, the colours you chose and your scores.
  • Your statistics: streak, best streak, average day score and days played. They are calculated on your device from your game history.
  • App state: such as whether you have seen the introduction and, if the app closed during a game, the round you were in.

Deleting the app removes this data from your iPhone, apart from any device backup you have turned on, such as iCloud Backup. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

3. No account, no server, no tracking

  • The app works without an internet connection. It does not connect to any server, ours or anyone else’s.
  • You do not need an account, and the app never asks for your name, email address or other personal details.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code, and does not use your device’s advertising identifier.
  • The daily colours are calculated on your device from the date, so every player gets the same puzzle without the app going online.

4. Sharing your result

Nothing leaves the app unless you choose to share it. When you tap a share button, the app creates an image or a short text with your puzzle number, your scores and the colours you chose, and opens the iOS share sheet. You decide where it goes. The app or service you share with handles it under its own privacy policy.

5. Photo library (add only)

If you choose Save Image in the share sheet, iOS asks whether the app may add photos to your library. The app uses this only to save your result card. This permission does not let the app see or read the photos in your library, and you can change it at any time in the Settings app.

6. Feedback and reviews

The app’s info screen has two buttons that open another app:

  • Send feedback opens your mail app with a new message to support@flux76.com. If you send it, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you and keep them no longer than needed to handle your question. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Leave a review opens the App Store. Ratings and reviews are handled by Apple under its own privacy policy.

7. Apple: App Store and TestFlight

Apple distributes the app through the App Store and, during the beta, through TestFlight. Apple processes data such as your Apple Account and your purchase and download history under its own privacy policy.

If you have chosen in your iPhone settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us crash reports and app usage statistics that do not identify you.

If you test the beta in TestFlight, the feedback, screenshots and crash reports you choose to send reach us through TestFlight, together with the information Apple includes with them. We use them only to improve the app.

8. Children

Colors of the Day is suitable for a general audience. Because the app collects no personal data, it does not collect personal data from children either.

9. Your rights

Because the app sends us nothing, we hold no personal data about you from the app itself. Your game history is under your control on your iPhone: delete the app to remove it.

If you have emailed us, you can ask to access, correct or delete that correspondence, and you have the other rights the GDPR gives you, such as restriction and objection. Email support@flux76.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority; in the Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy describes the app as it is today. If a future version changes how the app handles data, for example by adding an online feature or an in-app purchase, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date at the top.

11. Contact
