Colors of the Day Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Colors of the Day (shown as Color Day under the icon) is a daily color puzzle for iPhone, made by Flux76. This policy explains what data the app uses.

In short

  • You do not need an account and we do not know who you are.
  • The app has no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Almost everything stays on your phone. Only your daily score is sent, without a name, to compare you with other players.

What stays on your phone

Your played days, scores, streak, settings and the time of your daily reminder are stored only on your own device. The widget reads the same data on your device. If you delete the app, this data is gone.

The daily reminder is a notification that your phone schedules itself. Nothing is sent to a server for it.

What the app sends

When you finish a day, the app sends three things to our server:

  • your total score for that day (0 to 500);
  • the number of the day;
  • a random code that the app created itself when it was first used.

That code is not a name, email address, phone number or device number. It is not linked to anything else, and a reinstall gives you a new one.

Purpose. Only to show how your score compares with other players (“better than 83% of players”) and how many people played today. The code makes sure each player counts once per day.

Legal basis. Our legitimate interest in showing this comparison in the game, using data that cannot be traced to a person.

Where. Scores are stored in a database at Supabase, in the region Ireland. Supabase processes this data on our behalf. As with any internet request, the server technically sees your IP address; we do not store it with your score.

How long. Scores are kept so that statistics about earlier days can be shown. They contain nothing that lets us recognise you.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app sends your daily score and your best streak to Apple’s leaderboards. Apple manages that data under its own privacy policy. If you do not want this, sign out of Game Center in the Settings app on your iPhone.

Sharing and challenges

Your result is shared only when you choose to share it. The image or text then goes to the app you pick, such as Messages or WhatsApp. A challenge link contains the day number and the five round scores, and nothing else.

Contacting us

If you email us feedback, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply.

Children

The app does not ask for personal information and is not aimed at collecting data from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have, among others, the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, and to object to its use. Because the scores we receive are not linked to a person, we usually cannot tell which scores are yours. Contact us if you have questions about this; we will help as well as we can. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in the Netherlands: the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

If the app starts using other data, we will update this policy and change the date at the top.
